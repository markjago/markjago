About the tutor

A scholarly approach, taught plainly.

I’m a philosopher and a logician with over two decades of university lecturing experience. My private students range from sixth-formers preparing for university interviews to PhD candidates working through their thesis arguments — and a good many curious adults reading philosophy for the pleasure of it.

Tuition is one-to-one and built around the questions you actually want to answer. Sessions move at your pace: we read closely, argue carefully, and write clearly. The aim is always the same — to leave you thinking more rigorously than you did an hour earlier.

Alongside tutoring I run the Attic Philosophy YouTube channel, where I cover topics from formal logic to free will for a general audience — it’s a fair sample of the way I teach.
